# Test Lemon Spaghetti
A quick and refreshing citrus pasta dish.

## info  
* About 15 minutes  
* 2 servings  

## ingredients
* 200g spaghetti
* Salt (for pasta water)
* 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
* Zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* Fresh lemon juice
* Grated parmesan cheese
* Black pepper

## steps  
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water for 9 minutes.
2. In a skillet, warm extra virgin olive oil with lemon zest and minced garlic.
3. Drain pasta and toss into the skillet with fresh lemon juice, grated parmesan cheese, and black pepper.
