# Evelyn Rose's Honey Cake
A traditional spiced honey cake that improves with age.

## info  
* About 1 hour 15 minutes  
* 10 servings  

## ingredients
* 275g plain flour
* 75g caster sugar
* ½ tsp ground ginger
* ½ tsp ground cinnamon
* 1 level tsp mixed spice
* 225g clear honey
* 100ml flavourless oil
* 2 eggs
* 1 level tsp bicarbonate of soda
* 125ml fresh orange juice (about 1 ½ oranges)
* Grated zest of 1 orange
* 50g chopped walnuts

## steps  
1. Heat oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a tin about 25cm x 20cm x 5cm with baking parchment.
2. Mix together the flour, sugar, and spices in a large bowl. Make a well in the centre.
3. In a jug, mix the honey, oil, eggs, and orange zest and beat until smooth. Pour into the well and mix until you have a smooth batter.
4. Dissolve the bicarbonate of soda in the orange juice in a medium bowl. Add that and the chopped walnuts to the cake batter and stir quickly to combine.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and bake for 50–55 minutes, or until firm to the touch. Cool on a wire rack.

## notes  
* When cold, wrap in foil and leave at room temperature for 4–5 days before using.
* Can be frozen (well wrapped) for up to 3 months.
