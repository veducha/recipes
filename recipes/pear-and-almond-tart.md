# Pear and Almond Tart
Torta di Pere e Mandorle di Davide

## info  
* About 1 hour baking (+ 1 hour chilling)  
* 10–12 servings  

## ingredients
* 230g plain flour
* 115g unsalted cold butter, cut into cubes
* 50g icing sugar
* 2 egg yolks
* a pinch of salt

For the filling:
* 230g unsalted butter, softened
* 255g caster sugar
* 255g blanched whole almonds
* 2 eggs
* 6 ripe Comice pears, peeled and halved

## steps  
1. For the sweet pastry, pulse the flour, salt and butter in a food processor until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs. Add the sugar then the egg yolks and pulse. The mixture will immediately combine and leave the sides of the bowl. Remove, wrap in cling film and chill for at least an hour. Preheat the oven to 180°C/350°F/Gas 4.  
2. Coarsely grate the pastry into a 30 cm (12 in) loose-bottomed fluted flan tin, then press it evenly on to the sides and base. Bake blind for 20 minutes until very light brown. Cool, and place in the pears, face down. Reduce the oven temperature to 150°C/300°F/Gas 3.  
3. For the filling, cream the butter and sugar until the mixture is pale and light. Put the almonds in a food processor and chop until fine. Add the butter and sugar and blend, then beat in the eggs one by one. Pour over the pears and bake for for 40 minutes.
