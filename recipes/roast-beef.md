# Roast Beef
Joel's roast beef with homemade gravy

## info  
* About 1.5–2 hours  
* 1lb per person  


## ingredients
For the roast beef:
* Beef for roasting 
* A couple of onions (quartered)
* Olive oil
* Salt and pepper (or mustard is nice)

For the gravy:
* All the juices from the roast and the roasted onions
* 2 tablespoons of plain flour (dissolved in cold water)
* Beef stock (use a stock cube dissolved in 300mls boiling water – make this before hand)
* A teasp. of mustard (optional)
* Half cup of port / red wine (optional)
* Pepper (optional)
* Some mushrooms (optional)
* 1/2 teasp of brown sugar, or some red currant jelly or cranberry sauce (if wine has made it too tart)

## steps  
Go to the butchers:
* Ask for a succulent beef for roasting.
* Topside contains less fat, but I think it can be tough. 
* Sirloin is good. Fillet steak is the most expensive… 
* Order about 1lb/person


The roast beef:
1. Heat the oven to the highest setting (usually 200C).
2. Put the beef into a roasting pan.
3. Rub with olive oil, salt and pepper (or mustard is nice).
4. Put a couple of onions quartered next to the beef – this will help later to make a tasty gravy!
5. Put in the oven for 20min on the max temp, then reduce temp to 180’C and leave meat in oven 15mins for every 1lb of meat. I.e. for a 5lb roast, I would suggest 20min + 5x15min = 1.5hours.
6. Then take it out of the oven and let it rest for 20mins before carving. During this time, make the gravy…

The gravy for the roast beef:
1. Take the roast out of the roasting pan and let it rest on a wooden board.
2. Put the roasting pan with all the juices from the roast (with the roasted onions) on the hob – careful it is really hot, so hold it with oven gloves.
3. Dissolve 2 tablespoons of plain flour in cold water and gently pour this into the roasting pan, stirring all the time with a wooden spoon.
4. Now add the beef stock (use a stock cube dissolved in 300mls boiling water – make this before hand).
5. Stir all the time, scraping the sides of the roasting tin to get all the juicy bits into the gravy.
6. As it thickens, you can add a teasp. of mustard, and/or a half cup of port/ red wine, pepper, or some even mushrooms. Taste it – if the wine has made it too tart, a 1/2teasp of brown sugar will help – or some red currant jelly or cranberry sauce (what have you got in the fridge? – the onions will however have added sweetness).
7. Once the beef has been carved, pour a little of this delicious gravy over it to keep it MOIST!!! (your favourite word).

## notes  
* Serve with the veg that you like.
* Must include yorkies!
* Mustard and horse radish are my favourite condiments.
