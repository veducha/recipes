# Pavlova
Joel's party pavlova with cream and fresh berries

## info  
* 1 hour baking + overnight cooling  
* 8 servings  

## ingredients
* 6 large fresh egg whites
* 350g caster sugar
* Whipped cream OR Greek yoghurt
* Fruit – e.g. strawberries and raspberries
* Lightly oiled baking sheet (lining two baking trays)

## steps  
1. Preheat the oven to 140C. Line two baking trays with lightly oiled baking sheets.
2. Place the egg whites in a clean bowl and whisk so they form soft peaks and you can hold the bowl upside down without them sliding out!
3. Then slowly add the sugar very slowly a bit at a time.
4. Now divide the meringue into 2 and make a circle using a metal spoon create a slight dip in the middle so that the fruit will be able to sit in it and create fancy swirls around the rim.
5. Place the baking sheets in the oven and turn the heat down to 125’C.
6. Leave to bake for 1 hour.
7. Then switch off the oven – DO NOT BE TEMPTED TO OPEN IT.
8. Leave in the oven overnight! They will be fine in the morning.
9. Peel off the baking sheet.
10. Assemble with the cream/yoghurt and then fruit.
11. Then balance the 2nd meringue on top and repeat with the cream and fruit. The top fruits can be left whole in the same style as the others.

## notes  
* Leaving the meringues inside the turned-off oven overnight ensures they dry out perfectly with a crisp shell and soft marshmallow center.
