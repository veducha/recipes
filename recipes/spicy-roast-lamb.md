# Spicy Roast Lamb
Slow-cooked Middle Eastern style pulled lamb with fragrant spiced crust and gravy

## info  
* 4–5 hours  
* 6–8 servings  

## ingredients
* 1 leg or shoulder of lamb – 3.5kg
* 2 teasp coriander seeds
* 3 teasp cumin seeds
* 1.5 teasp ground cumin
* 2 teasp curry powder
* 1 tablesp thyme
* 1 tablesp chopped rosemary
* 4 cloves garlic squeezed
* 425mls chicken stock
* 2 tablesp fresh chopped coriander
* Lemon wedges (for serving)
* Olive oil
* Salt and pepper

## steps  
1. Combine all the herbs and spices in a bowl.
2. Brown the lamb on all sides in the oil in a heavy pot.
3. Pour in the stock.
4. Season meat with the salt and pepper.
5. Put the herb/spice mix carefully on the lamb - as a crust.
6. Cover with tight fitting lid and put in the oven at 150’C for 4-5 hours.
7. Remove from oven and place on warm plate – shred the meat.
8. Slightly reduce the juice to make the gravy.
9. Cover the lamb in the chopped coriander.
10. Serve with the gravy and lemon wedges….delicious and mouth watering!

## notes  
* This has a Middle Eastern flavour and goes well with butternut or sweet potato puree or even a Persian rice dish.
* Buy the lamb from the butcher – either shoulder or leg, perhaps the leg is less fatty!
* I like it when it has cooked for hours and just falls off the bone – so no need to carve – looks like ‘pulled lamb’.
