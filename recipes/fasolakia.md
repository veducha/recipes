# Fasolakia
Greek Green Beans in tomato sauce

## info  
* About 30 minutes  
* 4 servings  

## ingredients
* green beans	
* tomato	
* potatoes
* onion	
* cumin	
* paprika	
* white wine (optional)
* dried oregano

## steps  
1. In a large Dutch oven, heat half of the olive oil over medium. Add the onion and cook until tender, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes until softened.
1. Then add the garlic, cumin, and oregano. Cook another 2 minutes, stirring regularly.
1. Add tomatoes, white wine (or water), bay leaf, green beans beans, and potatoes. Season with salt and pepper. Raise the heat and bring to a high simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
1. Cover and transfer to the heated oven (or in the stove at gentle temperature). Cook until the sauce slightly thickens, and the beans and potatoes can easily be cut with side of your fork; about 35 minutes or so. 
1. Season and serve. Remove from oven and stir in the lemon juice, parsley, and remaining olive oil. Enjoy!

## notes  
* You can also add a spoonful of tahini for extra creaminess
