# Lentejas a la Margarita
Las lentejas de toda la vida

## info  
* About 60 minutes  
* 4 servings  

## ingredients
* lentils	
* 1/2 chorizo ring
* 1 red bell pepper 
* 2 tomatoes	
* 2 potatoes	
* 2 carrots 	
* paprika	
* garlic

## steps  
1. Fry the chorizo until it releases the red oil
1. Add the onion and garlic and fry until translucent and soft
1. Add the rest of the ingredients roughly chopped, including the paprika (a good amount!) and cover with water
1. Let simmer gently for 1 hour.
