# Aubergine Chermoula
Sweet and spiced North African aubergine relish with raisins, tomatoes, and warm spices

## info  
* About 45 minutes  
* 6 servings (as part of a mezze)  

## ingredients
* 2 aubergines (trimmed and cut into 2.5cm cubes)
* 1/2 large onion (thinly sliced into half moons)
* 3 garlic cloves (crushed into a paste with salt)
* 1/2 red pepper (cored, deseeded, cut into strips)
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
* 1 tsp sweet paprika
* Handful of plump golden raisins
* 400g can chopped tomatoes
* 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
* 2 tsp caster sugar
* 2 tbsp clear honey
* Olive oil
* Sea salt

## steps  
1. Heat a good glug of olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the aubergine cubes and fry until golden brown, seasoning with sea salt. Once nicely browned, remove from the pan and set aside.
2. Return the pan to the heat and reduce to low. Add the sliced onion and cook until caramelized, stirring so they colour evenly.
3. Just as the onions start to brown, stir in the garlic paste, cumin, cinnamon, and sweet paprika. Add the red pepper strips and continue sautéing until softened and no longer raw.
4. Add the golden raisins and a pinch of salt; cook for 5 minutes. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for another 5 minutes, then stir in the red wine vinegar, caster sugar, and honey.
5. Increase the heat and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes (ensuring it doesn't burn). Reduce heat back to low, and lightly mash the sauce with a potato masher.
6. Stir in the cooked aubergine. Simmer gently over low heat until the aubergine is very soft.
7. Take off the heat and leave to cool.

## notes  
* You need to cook the aubergine for long until it is very soft.
* Delicious served warm or at room temperature as part of a mezze spread.
