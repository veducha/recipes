# Chocolate Almond Torte
Rich flourless torte dusted with icing sugar

## info  
* About 1 hour  
* 8–10 servings  

## ingredients
* 225g ground almonds
* 225g bitter chocolate (ground in food processor)
* 225g unsalted butter
* 225g sugar
* 6 medium eggs (separated)
* Tiny bit of oil (for lining the tin)
* Icing sugar (for dusting)

## steps  
1. Preheat the oven to 145C.
2. Line a torte baking tray with baking sheet cut to size and rub with tiny bit of oil.
3. Cream the butter and sugar in the food processor/electric mixer.
4. Add the egg yolks one at a time with the food processor/electric mixer on.
5. Then add the ground chocolate and the ground almonds.
6. Beat the egg whites stiffly in a separate clean bowl to form soft peaks.
7. Fold the chocolate mixture into the beaten egg whites – carefully, not too vigorously – you don’t want to lose all the air you have beaten in.
8. Pour the mixture into the tin and bake for about 42 minutes.
9. Switch the oven off and leave slightly open before taking torte out so that it acclimatizes to the temperature – otherwise it sinks.
10. When cold remove from tin carefully and sift over icing sugar.

## notes
* Serve with vanilla ice-cream OR raspberry coulis OR orange sorbet OR anything you like!!
* Naturally gluten-free.
