# Tuna and White Bean Salad
With quick-pickled red onion, fresh herbs, and sliced tomatoes

## info  
* About 15 minutes  
* 2 servings  

## ingredients
* 150g preserved tuna in olive oil
* 300g cooked white beans
* 1 red onion
* 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
* 5g parsley
* 3 tbsp of good extra virgin olive oil
* 4 different types of tomato as the base (sliced into large circles)
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## steps  
1. Slice the onion in half, remove the skin and then slice it really nice and fine. Add it to a bowl, along with the vinegar and salt. Give it a good mix and leave it to one side for about 5 minutes for a quick pickle.
2. Finely chop the fresh parsley. Drain the tuna and the beans, add these to the onions followed by the parsley and a drizzle of good extra virgin olive oil. Give it a good stir all together, breaking down the tuna a little bit with the spoon as you go. Ideally you want to leave this overnight or for at least a few hours in the fridge but it's also delicious eaten right away.
3. Slice the tomatoes into large circles and place them around the bottom of the serving plate as the base. Drizzle with a touch of olive oil and a sprinkle of salt.
4. Serve the tuna, beans and onion mixture on top and garnish with some more fresh parsley leaves.
