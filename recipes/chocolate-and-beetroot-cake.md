# Chocolate and Beetroot Cake
Rich, fudgy cake made with dark chocolate, beetroot, and ground almonds

## info  
* About 60 minutes  
* 8 servings  

## ingredients
* 200g butter
* 200g dark chocolate (70%)
* 250g cooked beetroot
* 5 eggs
* 250g dark brown sugar
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
* 240g ground almonds
* Pinch of salt

## steps  
1. Preheat the oven to 190°C. Line a 20cm cake tin with greaseproof baking paper.
2. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together in a heatproof bowl, then leave to cool.
3. In a large bowl, blitz the cooked beetroot with a hand blender until smooth.
4. Blend in the eggs, dark brown sugar, baking powder, and vanilla extract until well combined.
5. Pour in the melted chocolate and butter mixture and fully blend.
6. Add the ground almonds and a pinch of salt, blending until evenly incorporated.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and bake for 35–45 minutes, until just set and slightly springy to the touch.
8. Allow to cool before slicing and serving.

## notes  
* Naturally gluten-free using ground almonds in place of wheat flour. 
* Best served with a dollop of greek yoghurt or sour cream, and some berries.
