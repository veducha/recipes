# Moussaka
Traditional Greek baked dish with spiced beef, layered vegetables, and creamy béchamel

## info  
* About 90 minutes  
* 6–8 servings  

## ingredients
For the vegetables:
* 3 potatoes (boiled, sliced into 1 cm rounds)
* 5–6 tbsp olive oil
* 1 onion (thinly sliced)
* 2 medium zucchini (sliced)
* 2 aubergine (sliced)
* Fresh thyme
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper

For the minced meat:
* 500g minced beef
* 1 onion (coarsely chopped)
* 1 clove garlic (finely chopped)
* 2 tbsp olive oil (for sautéing)
* 1 tbsp tomato paste
* 400g crushed tomatoes
* 1 tsp ground cinnamon
* 1/2 tsp grated nutmeg
* 3 pinches granulated sugar
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper

For the béchamel sauce:
* 100g butter
* 100g all-purpose flour
* 750ml whole milk
* 100g parmesan (grated)
* 3 egg yolks
* 1 pinch grated nutmeg
* Freshly ground black pepper

For assembly & topping:
* 50g parmesan (grated)

## steps  
For the vegetables:
1. Brush the potato and aubergine (and zuchinni) slices with olive oil and bake in the oven until golden.

For the minced meat:
1.  In a pan over high heat, brown the minced beef until golden brown. Don't worry about burnt bits (they add flavour!). Remove from the pan.
1.  In the same pan, sauté the chopped onion and garlic in olive oil.
2. Add the tomato paste, sugar, nutmeg, and cinnamon. Scrape the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon to cook off the paste and deepen the flavor.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, reduce heat to low, and simmer gently for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season with salt and pepper just before removing from heat.

For the béchamel sauce:
1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat. Add the flour and whisk continuously to form a smooth roux.
2. Gradually add the milk in small portions, whisking constantly to ensure no lumps form. Allow each addition to be fully absorbed before adding the next.
3. Continue stirring until the sauce begins to bubble gently and becomes thick, creamy, and smooth.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the grated nutmeg, black pepper, grated parmesan, and egg yolks until velvety.

Assembly and baking:
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan mode).
2. Stir about 200g of the prepared béchamel sauce into the meat sauce to bind the filling together. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper if needed.
3. Spread the minced meat mixture evenly over the layered vegetables in the baking dish.
4. Pour the remaining béchamel over the top, spreading it evenly to all edges, and sprinkle with 50g grated parmesan.
5. Bake for 35–40 minutes until golden brown on top.
6. Remove from the oven and let rest and cool thoroughly for about 1 hour so the layers set properly before slicing and serving.

## notes  
* Resting for a full hour before slicing is crucial for clean squares that hold their shape.
