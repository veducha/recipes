# Test Carbonara
A classic, comforting pasta dish with pancetta, eggs, and freshly grated cheese.

## info  
* About 20 minutes  
* 4 servings  

## ingredients
* 100g pancetta, rind removed and finely chopped
* 50g pecorino cheese, finely grated
* 50g parmesan, finely grated
* 3 large eggs
* 350g spaghetti
* 2 plump garlic cloves, peeled and bruised
* 50g unsalted butter
* Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

## steps  
1. Put a large saucepan of water on to boil.
2. Finely chop the pancetta and mix the finely grated pecorino and parmesan together in a bowl.
3. Beat the eggs in a medium bowl and season with a little freshly grated black pepper. Set aside.
4. Add 1 tsp salt and 350g spaghetti to the boiling water, then cook at a constant simmer for about 10 minutes or until al dente.
5. Squash the peeled garlic cloves with the flat blade of a knife to bruise them.
6. Drop the butter into a large frying pan or wok and, once melted, add the pancetta and bruised garlic.
7. Cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until the pancetta is golden and crisp. Remove and discard the garlic cloves.
8. Reduce heat under the pancetta to low. When the pasta is cooked, lift it directly into the frying pan using tongs, reserving the pasta water.
9. Mix most of the cheese into the beaten egg mixture, keeping a small handful reserved for serving.
10. Take the pan of spaghetti and pancetta off the heat. Quickly pour in the egg and cheese mixture, tossing the spaghetti with tongs until coated and thick.
11. Add several tablespoons of reserved pasta cooking water to create a moist, saucy consistency. Season with salt if needed.
12. Divide pasta among serving plates and serve immediately topped with the reserved cheese and a grating of black pepper.

## notes  
* If the dish gets dry before serving, splash in a bit more hot pasta water to revive the glossy sauce.
