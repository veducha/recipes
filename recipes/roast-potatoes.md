# Roast Potatoes
Fluffy on the inside and crispy on the outside

## info  
* About 1 hour 20 minutes  
* 2–4 potatoes per person  

## ingredients
* 2-4 potatoes per person (either Maris Piper, Desirée or King Edward)
* 120ml vegetable/sunflower oil (not olive oil)
* 2 tsp salt

## steps  
1. Peel the potatoes and cut into 1/8ths (or pieces that are about 3cms across).
2. Put into water with a tsp of salt and boil.
3. Drain them after they have actually boiled for 3mins.
4. When they are drained and dry, put the lid on the pot and give it a really good shake – this will make them fluffy and crispy.
5. Now, remove the lid of the pot and they can just sit there until you are ready to roast them (the reason for removing the lid is so that they do not sweat - or become moist – you love that word!!).
6. 1hr and 20mins before you are ready to eat, take a large baking tray, put vegetable oil into it – about 120mls with a good tsp. of salt and put it into a very hot oven – 200’C, fan preheated and let the oil get nice and hot – about 6mins or so.
7. Now carefully remove the hot tray with the heated oil from the oven and place on the hob which is on and very carefully put the potatoes into the very hot salty oil – they will sizzle.
8. Let the potatoes slightly brown on the hob, turn them carefully so they are basted in the oil.
9. After a few mins. place the tray with the potatoes back into the oven. You can reduce the temp to 190C. Let them roast and move them about every 20mins or so so that they evenly browned and crispy.
10. They will be delicious and ready in about 1hour 10mins.
11. Remove them from the hot oil with a slotted or mesh spoon so that the excess oil is left behind.
12. Put them in a heated bowl. Enjoy!

## notes  
* Depending how greedy you are, how many people you are cooking for and possibly how hungry you think they might be, use between 2 and 4 potatoes per person!
